# Photography credits

The wallpapers in `backgrounds/` are colour-graded derivatives of a SpaceX Starship launch photograph. The photograph remains © SpaceX and is not covered by the theme's MIT licence. SpaceX publishes its media usage policy at [spacex.com/trademark](https://www.spacex.com/trademark). This is an independent, non-commercial community theme, not endorsed by SpaceX.

`1-postcard` through `4-iron-horse` were remapped with [lutgen](https://github.com/ozwaldorf/lutgen-rs). `0-frontier-print` is an albumen-print grade.

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the colour palette, configuration files, ports, scripts and `assets/palette.svg`. It excludes the images in `backgrounds/`.
